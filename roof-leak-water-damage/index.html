Roof Leak Water Damage Help in Conway

The spot on your ceiling is seldom directly below the hole in the roof. A loose shingle, a failed flashing joint or an aging seal around a skylight lets rain in, and it travels across the roof deck and framing until it falls into the insulation. Often the attic has been damp for some time before any brown mark reaches the room below. We find the full wet area and dry it out.

To be clear, roof repair is not what we do, water damage is. We work on the interior, which means removing wet insulation, drying drywall and framing, dealing with mold and rebuilding the ceiling. If rain is still coming in, we can tarp the roof as a temporary cover, or work alongside the roofer you choose so the leak is fixed before we close anything up.

Do This First

Your First Steps

  1. Cut Power to Wet FixturesIf water is dripping through a light fixture, a ceiling fan or near an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker and leave the fixture alone.
  2. Catch the DripsSet buckets or pans under active drips and move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the way. Protect the floor with plastic sheeting or old towels.
  3. Drain a Bulging CeilingWhen water pools in a bubble overhead, pierce the low point with a screwdriver while standing clear, and let it run into a bucket.
  4. Leave the Roof AloneA wet roof and an unlit attic are both places to stay out of. Just observe the entry point from the room below, then hand the job to a trained crew.
  5. Document What You SeeBefore anything is moved or discarded, get video and photos of the attic, the ceiling and damaged items, and then phone us so drying can begin.
Insurance

Insurance and Roof Leak Water Damage

Homeowners policies often cover interior water damage when a roof leak is sudden and accidental, such as wind tearing off shingles or a limb puncturing the roof. A leak put down to an old, worn or neglected roof is usually denied, and fixing the roof may be treated separately from the interior work. Photos and moisture readings from us go to the adjuster, though only your policy decides coverage, and your agent can explain it.

How We Fix It

Our Roof Leak Water Damage Process

We inspect the attic, ceilings and wall cavities with moisture meters and thermal imaging to map everywhere the rain traveled, including areas that still look dry from the room below.

The Usual Culprits

Why This Happens

  • Rubber boots around plumbing vent pipes that split after years in the sun.
  • Leaves and grit collecting where two roof slopes meet, damming rain until it seeps through.
  • Popped or exposed roofing nails, each one a tiny opening for rain.
  • Storm impact, such as hail or a tree branch, breaking through the shingles.
  • Gutters packed with leaves, which push water back beneath the first row of shingles.
What Is at Risk

What the Water Damages

Drywall Ceilings

Drywall that stays wet stains, sags and loses its strength. Joints open, tape peels, and a waterlogged piece may fall without much notice.

Hidden Attic Mold

A dark, warm attic with damp wood and paper-faced drywall suits mold well. It may take hold in 24 to 48 hours and work its way over the underside of the sheathing.

Wall Cavities

From the attic, a leak can drop into a stud bay and soak the wall insulation and drywall from behind. You might notice nothing more than a peeling patch or a puffy baseboard.

Ceiling Fixtures and Wiring

Because fixtures are holes in the drywall, a leak often shows up at a light or fan first. Wet connections can short or corrode, so the circuit should stay off until it is checked.
