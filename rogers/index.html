Bathrooms: A tub left filling, a sink with a blocked overflow hole or a toilet that runs over can soak a bathroom floor quickly. Walls: Plumbing hidden in a wall may seep at a joint for weeks without leaving a puddle anywhere. Tanks: A water heater holds a large volume under pressure, usually in a closet, garage or utility room.
